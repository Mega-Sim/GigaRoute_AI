Humanoid_Define_Obstacle(name, x1_m, y1_m, x2_m, y2_m [, level_id [, z_min_m [, z_max_m]]])

Purpose
Defines a rectangular obstacle Humanoids walk around (optionally limited to a height range).
Syntax
call Humanoid_Define_Obstacle(name, x1_m, y1_m, x2_m, y2_m [, level_id [, z_min_m [, z_max_m]]])
Example
call Humanoid_Define_Obstacle(name, x1_m, y1_m, x2_m, y2_m [, level_id [, z_min_m [, z_max_m]]])
Notes
On a level with at least one obstacle, humanoid_walk_to/walk/transfer plan a detour (AMR grid A*, body half-width + 50 mm clearance) and use its length x route_factor. Levels without obstacles keep straight-line x route_factor. A walk with no route is rejected. Already planned walks are not re-planned. Height range (default floor to ceiling): an obstacle blocks a Humanoid only if z_max > 0.1 m and z_min < its height, so a low curb is stepped over and an overhead beam is walked under. AMR positions at request time are treated as temporary obstacles on the walk. Implementation spelling: humanoid_define_obstacle().

Humanoid_Define_Zone(name, x1_m, y1_m, x2_m, y2_m [, capacity [, level_id]])

Purpose
Defines a corridor/area that at most `capacity` (default 1) Humanoids may be walking through at once.
Syntax
call Humanoid_Define_Zone(name, x1_m, y1_m, x2_m, y2_m [, capacity [, level_id]])
Example
call Humanoid_Define_Zone(name, x1_m, y1_m, x2_m, y2_m [, capacity [, level_id]])
Notes
A Humanoid entering from outside waits at the entrance until the zone has room (first-requested first-served). Only walking time inside the zone is counted; standing Humanoids and other vehicle types do not occupy it. Zones on one level must not overlap. Implementation spelling: humanoid_define_zone().
